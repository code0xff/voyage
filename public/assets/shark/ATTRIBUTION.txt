Changes made, as CC BY 4.0 requires them to be stated:

  * The mesh is compressed with EXT_meshopt_compression and its vertex
    attributes are quantized (KHR_mesh_quantization). This is a change of
    storage, not of shape: the same 73,986 vertices and 30,020 triangles, to
    within the quantization step. It takes the file from 3.9 MB to 509 kB.

There are no textures to re-encode; this model is materials only. It is also
scaled, positioned and animated at runtime by voyage. Its geometry, its
skinning and its animation are otherwise as downloaded, and nothing has been
added to or removed from it.
