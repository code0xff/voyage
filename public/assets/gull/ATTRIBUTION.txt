Changes made, as CC BY 4.0 requires them to be stated:

  * The mesh is compressed with EXT_meshopt_compression and its vertex
    attributes are quantized (KHR_mesh_quantization). This is a change of
    storage, not of shape: the same 740 vertices and 796 triangles, to within
    the quantization step.
  * The embedded textures are re-encoded to WebP at quality 88
    (EXT_texture_webp). Same pixel dimensions; the encoding is lossy.
  * Together these take the file from 370 kB to 77 kB.

Voyage also scales and positions the flock at runtime and stretches one
authored circuit of the animation over each sighting. Its geometry, its
skinning and its animation are otherwise as downloaded, and nothing has been
added to or removed from it.
