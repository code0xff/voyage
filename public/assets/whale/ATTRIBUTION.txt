Changes made, as CC BY 4.0 requires them to be stated:

  * The mesh is compressed with EXT_meshopt_compression and its vertex
    attributes are quantized (KHR_mesh_quantization). This is a change of
    storage, not of shape: the same 1,554 vertices and 2,756 triangles, to
    within the quantization step.
  * The embedded textures are re-encoded from PNG to WebP at quality 88
    (EXT_texture_webp). Same pixel dimensions; the encoding is lossy.
  * Together these take the file from 821 kB to 119 kB.

The model is also scaled, positioned and animated at runtime by voyage. Its
geometry, its skinning and its animation are otherwise as downloaded, and
nothing has been added to or removed from it.
